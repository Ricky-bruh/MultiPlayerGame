Common questions.

Is Pat Pay secure?

Yes. Pat Pay uses industry-leading security with end-to-end encryption and doesn't store your actual card details on your device. Every transaction is protected by your biometric authentication.

Where can I use Pat Pay?

Pat Pay works at any terminal that accepts contactless payments worldwide. This includes most major retailers, restaurants, transit systems, and online merchants.

How do I add my cards?

Simply open Pat Pay, tap "Add Card," and either scan your card with your camera or enter the details manually. Verification is typically instant for most major banks and card issuers.

Are there any fees?

None. Pat Pay is completely free for consumers. We don't charge any transaction fees, monthly fees, or hidden costs.

What devices are supported?

Pat Pay works on any Android device running Android 8.0 (Oreo) or newer with NFC capability. This includes most smartphones and smartwatches released after 2017.
